Soul Rush: playtest questionnaire

For private playtesters. It takes about 10 minutes (the "2-minute version" at the end is enough if they are short on time). Copy it into Google Forms, Typeform or a shared document: the scales are 1 to 5 unless stated, and every section ends with an optional free-text box.

For a real form (scales, choices, tick boxes, answers collected in a spreadsheet) use docs/playtest-form.gs instead: see the steps in that file. This document is the reading copy of the same questions.

Before sending, fill in the build line at the top of the form. Testers should not have to guess which version they played.

Intro for the testers

Thanks for playing Soul Rush, a boss-rush action game about deflects and posture. Break a boss's posture, then land the deathblow. There are no right or wrong answers, and "this felt unfair" or "I did not understand this" is exactly what I need to hear. Please answer from what you felt, not what you think I want to hear. You can skip any question.

1. About you

  1. How often do you play action games with dodges or blocks (Souls-likes, Sekiro, Hades, Hollow Knight, Dark Souls)?
  • Never / A little / Regularly / A lot
  1. Have you played a game built around deflecting or parrying (Sekiro, Nioh, Hi-Fi Rush)?
  • Yes / No
  1. What did you play on? (Keyboard / Controller / Both) and in which browser?
  2. How long did you play? (Under 15 min / 15 to 30 / 30 to 60 / Over an hour)
  3. Which fights did you reach? (Tutorial / Samurai phase 1 / phase 2 / phase 3 / Leaf Ranger / Gunslinger / beat one or more bosses)

2. First impression and the tutorial

  1. After the tutorial, did you know what to do in the first fight? (1 = no idea, 5 = completely)
  2. Which of these did the tutorial teach clearly? (tick all)
  • Moving and rolling / Attacking / Deflecting / The posture bar / The deathblow / Lives and the curse
  1. Which of those was still unclear after the tutorial?
  2. Was the tutorial too long, too short, or about right?
  3. Did any prompt or instruction confuse you? Which one?

3. Controls and mechanics

Rate how each felt (1 = bad, 5 = great), and add a note if it was a 1 or 2.

  1. Movement (walking and running)
  2. Attack (the sword combo)
  3. Roll (the dodge and its invulnerability)
  4. Deflect (raising the sword just as a hit lands)
  5. Lunge (Q, the stamina-costing dash attack)
  6. Deathblow (F on a posture-broken boss)
  1. Stamina: attacks cost stamina (15 each) as do rolls and lunges. Which is closest?
  • I almost never ran out / I sometimes ran out and it felt right / I ran out too often and it felt unfair / I did not notice stamina
  1. Directional deflect: a deflect only works if you face the attack. How did that feel?
  • Natural / Fine once I got used to it / Confusing / Frustrating
  1. Deflect timing window: the window for a deflect is short. Was it:
  • Too generous / About right / A bit too tight / Far too tight
  1. Did the controls ever do something you did not intend (an attack when you wanted a roll, a deflect that did not come out)? Describe.
  2. Is there a move or key you never used? Which one and why?

4. Does the game feel fluid?

  1. Overall, how responsive were the controls? (1 = sluggish, 5 = immediate)
  2. Was there a delay between pressing a key and the character reacting?
  • Never / Sometimes / Often / Always
  1. How was the frame rate?
  • Smooth throughout / Mostly smooth with some drops / Often choppy / Unplayable
  1. When did it stutter? (tick all)
  • Loading a scene / A boss fight / Specific attacks or effects / Around the hub / Not at all
  1. Did sounds line up with what you saw (hit, deflect, attack)?
  • Yes / Mostly / Sounds came late / Sounds were missing
  1. Did the hit-stop and screen shake when you hit or deflect feel good, too much, or too little?
  2. Anything that felt stiff, floaty, slow to recover or "sticky"?

5. Difficulty and fairness

  1. Overall difficulty (1 = far too easy, 3 = just right, 5 = far too hard).
  2. How fair did the game feel? (1 = it often felt cheap, 5 = every death was my fault)
  3. Deaths: when you died, was it usually (pick one)
  • I made a mistake and knew it / I did not understand what killed me / It felt unavoidable / A bug
  1. Telegraphs: could you read enemy attacks before they hit? (1 = no, 5 = always)
  2. Which attack was hardest to read? (Tell us the boss and what it looked like.)
  3. Did any attack feel impossible to deflect or dodge? Which?
  4. Damage: hits do real damage and also build your posture bar. Were hits
  • Too weak / About right / Too punishing
  1. Lives and curse: each death costs max HP until you land a deathblow. Did you understand it, and did it feel
  • Motivating / Neutral / Too harsh / Confusing
  1. Healing (rally): hitting a boss soon after being hurt gives some health back. Did you notice it?
  • Yes and I used it / Yes but it did not matter / No
  1. Did you ever feel "this fight is simply too long"? Which one and at what point?
  2. Did you ever feel "this fight is too easy"? Which one?

6. Posture and the deathblow

  1. Did you understand what the boss's posture bar does? (1 = not at all, 5 = fully)
  2. How do you break a boss's posture most of the time? (tick all)
  • Deflecting his attacks / Hitting him with the sword / Both / I could not break it
  1. How did breaking posture feel?
  • Satisfying and earned / Too easy / Too slow / Impossible to keep up
  1. Did you understand your own posture bar (the one under the arena)? When it filled, did you know why?
  2. Was the stun when your posture broke (about 2 seconds) fair?
  3. Landing the deathblow: satisfying, too easy, too hard to reach?

7. The bosses

Answer for the bosses you played. For each: Fun (1 to 5), Fair (1 to 5), Readable (1 to 5), and one sentence.

The Samurai

  1. Fun / Fair / Readable (1 to 5 each)
  2. Phase 1 (plain swings): difficulty (1 easy, 5 hard)
  3. Phase 2 (he switches sides each attack): difficulty (1 to 5)
  4. Phase 3 (faster, with afterimages): difficulty (1 to 5)
  5. The afterimages: the images that appear after a dodge, a dash strike or a wall slash and attack on their own. Pick one.
  • Cool and readable / Cool but confusing / Too many at once / I did not notice them
  1. Facing the image: in phase 3 you have to face the image, not him, to deflect its attack. Did you work that out?
  • Yes right away / After a while / No
  1. Dodging: the Samurai dodges some of your attacks. Pick one.
  • Fine, it keeps him alive / Too often / Too rarely / Made him feel untouchable
  1. The grab (he reaches for you, cannot be deflected): fair, too hard, or too easy to avoid?
  2. The glints (red eye glint before a dash, red glint on the sheath before the wall slash): did they warn you in time? (1 to 5)
  3. Petals, the tornado and the Judgement Cut: which was hardest, and which was your favorite?
  4. Anything in his pattern that felt random, even though it is meant to be fixed and learnable?

  4. The sniper perch (he leaps up and fires arrows, only a deflected laser brings him down): clear, confusing or too hard?

8. The hub, story and presentation

  1. The hub: was it clear where to go and how to start a fight? (1 to 5)
  2. Training question from the keeper (yes or no): helpful, annoying, or unnoticed?
  3. Visuals: how well could you read the screen in the middle of a fight? (1 = a mess, 5 = clear)
  4. Were there effects, backgrounds or particles that were distracting or hid an attack?
  5. Audio: music (1 to 5), sound effects (1 to 5). Anything too loud, too quiet or repetitive?
  6. Did the sword clashes and deflects sound satisfying?
  7. UI: could you always read your HP, stamina and posture, and the boss's HP and posture? Anything hard to see?

9. Overall

  1. Would you keep playing this game? (1 = no, 5 = definitely)
  2. Would you recommend it to a friend who likes challenging action games?
  3. What was the best moment?
  4. What was the most frustrating moment?
  5. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
  6. Bugs: anything broken, stuck or glitchy? Where and what were you doing? (Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.)
  7. Anything else?

The 2-minute version

If a tester only has a couple of minutes, ask just these:

  • Overall difficulty (1 to 5) and fairness (1 to 5): questions 29 and 30
  • How responsive did the controls feel? Question 22
  • Frame rate and sound delay: questions 24 and 26
  • Could you read the attacks? Question 32
  • Best moment, most frustrating moment, one thing to change: questions 73 to 75
